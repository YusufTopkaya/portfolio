# Twingo Racer — Meta (title screen, audio, highscores, brackets)

- **Twingo Racer title screen**: the game overlay now opens on a retro key-art title screen before the engine boots — `public/images/twingo-title-mobile.webp` (portrait, 941×1672) and `twingo-title-desktop.webp` (landscape, 1639×960), picked via `<picture>` + `(min-aspect-ratio: 1/1)`. The painted START / LEADERBOARD buttons are transparent %-positioned hit areas in an aspect-locked box (`.racer-title-art` in `app/globals.css`) — if the artwork is ever regenerated, those coordinates must be re-measured. Keyboard: ↑/↓ arms a button, Enter/Space activates, ESC closes. LEADERBOARD opens a read-only top-10 panel (plain `GET /api/highscore`, no token). The engine + submit-token issuance are gated on leaving the title screen, so idling there is free. **TODAY'S TRACK difficulty card**: `components/retro/racer/trackstats.ts` rates the first ~3 km of the daily layout (a second `createTrackGenerator` replayed from the same UTC-day seed, read in ≤1200-segment chunks because 3 km ≈ 3600 segments overflows the 2048-slot ring) on three 1-5 star axes — CURVES (mean |curve|), HILLS (total climb), FUEL (mean |curve| at the can spots — cans parked on hard bends are harder to grab) — plus a composite verdict stamp (CHILL / BALANCED / TWISTY / HILLY / CRUEL). DOM chrome stays clear of ALL painted content: the SETTINGS chip is a compact button at top-right just left of the ✕ (`top: 10px; right: 50px`), and the difficulty card is a small vertical card in the top-left sky corner (`top: 10px; left: 12px`) — the art's buttons and its PRESS ENTER/© footer are all at the bottom, the logo starts lower and further right. Hidden while a title panel is open; dev consoles get the raw values for threshold tuning. On touch devices the whole overlay goes borderless full-bleed: `@media (pointer: coarse)` zeroes `--racer-bezel-x/y`, drops the CRT chin and shell padding/radius, so the glass fills the screen edge-to-edge (the desktop monitor frame is untouched). Viewport sizing: never `100vh` (mobile vh is the URL-bar-hidden height — a vh-sized canvas is taller than the visible viewport and the centred layout clips the LCD cluster off the top). The title art uses `100dvh`, and even that proved unreliable on some phones (dvh reporting the large height with the bar showing), so on touch devices the game canvas is fitted in JS instead: a `canvasCss` state measured from `window.visualViewport` (re-fit on window + visualViewport resize, re-run when `buf` flips orientation), preserving the buffer aspect inside the visible box exactly; desktop keeps the pure-CSS `min(100vw, 100dvh·aspect)` formula.
- **Twingo Racer audio**: `components/retro/racer/audio.ts` synthesizes everything with raw Web Audio (no assets). The engine voice is a Mk1 D7F firing model — 1149 cc inline-4 four-stroke, f = RPM/30 (28 Hz idle → 200 Hz redline; sub sine + main saw + dominant 2nd harmonic + phase-locked sub-octave saw through a resonant lowpass into a throttle-load-driven waveshaper for growl, plus filtered-noise rumble); lifting off at revs fires bandpassed overrun-crackle pops, and at the gear top with the throttle pinned a rev limiter stutters the ignition in an irregular cut pattern; `EngineState.rpm01` is wheel-speed over the gear's top, so upshifts drop revs by the ratio gap and downshifts kick them up on their own — the 0.28 s shift cut (`GEAR_TOPS`/`SHIFT_TIME` in `engine.ts`, committed gear + aero-only coast during the cut) only ducks load and fires a clutch thump. Acceleration follows the real D7F (0-100 in ~13.4 s, ~20 s more to 150), with an arcade tail to the 180 speedo ceiling. Bandpass-noise brake screech and tire skid (`EngineState.skid`/`braking`), and a synthwave loop that adds layers by score (lead ≥12.5k, octave doubler ≥30k, double-time hats ≥50k (halved with the ×0.5 score scale)). Three buses (music/engine/menu) sit under the master mute; a SETTINGS panel (title-screen chip + pause-menu option, `.racer-settings` in `globals.css`) sets each 0-10, persisted in `localStorage("twingo:vol")`. The car is silent while paused (`setPaused`, music keeps playing) and after game over; `start()` guards the `AudioContext` constructor (`window.AudioContext ?? webkitAudioContext`, no-op when absent) — a bare `new AudioContext()` throws a ReferenceError on WebAudio-less engines and used to crash the whole component into the error boundary on overlay open; a hidden tab / locked phone suspends the WHOLE AudioContext (`setHidden` wired to `visibilitychange` in `TwingoRacer.tsx`, alongside the existing auto-pause + key-drop) so nothing plays while the game isn't visible — the wake is NOT resumed inside the visibilitychange handler (tab-return frames are the jankiest the browser produces); `setHidden(false)` only arms `hiddenDeferred` and the next user-driven entry point (`drive`/`menuMove`/`menuSelect` → `ensureRunning`) pays the resume, while the music scheduler's lookahead just waits out the freeze. Tab-return jank probe (dev only): the game loop in `TwingoRacer.tsx` captures ~5 s of raw frame intervals after any >250 ms wake gap and logs an avg/p95/max summary, and the FPS overlay's 0.5 s window is reset on every wake so it never averages the freeze into a bogus single-digit reading. Braking also lights the car sprite's stop lamps (chase view, drawn after the sprite in `engine.ts` render). Boost adds its own voice on the engine bus: a swept-bandpass NOS whoosh + a sub-bass punch (130→45 Hz sine drop) on activation, then a detuned sawtooth growl (65→195 Hz through a rev-following lowpass) + lowpassed air roar under a faint whistle while `boostT` burns (the roar is the audible countdown), and a blow-off "psshh" (3400→1300 Hz) on the falling edge — `drive()` takes a `boost` flag from `TwingoRacer`; pause/game-over/stop all zero the boost gain. Dev only: `window.__twingo` exposes the engine for e2e probes.
- **Twingo Racer highscores**: `/api/highscore` (`app/api/highscore/route.ts` + `lib/highscore.ts`) stores scores in `data/highscores.json` (override with `HIGHSCORE_FILE`). Retention: all-time top-50 plus everything from the last 40 days (hard cap 300) so the period boards have history. `GET ?period=all|monthly|weekly|daily` returns the top-10 over TR-CALENDAR-DAY-aligned windows (Turkey is permanently UTC+3, `trDay`/`trDayStart` in `lib/highscore.ts`) — the 24H board resets at midnight TR (the same moment the daily track flips), 7D/30D advance day-by-day on the TR clock; they are NOT rolling hour windows — the game shows these as ALL/30D/7D/24H tabs on the title and game-over leaderboards (selection persisted in `localStorage("twingo:board-period")`; after a submit the tab pins to ALL because the returned rank is all-time, and a generation guard (`boardGenRef` in `TwingoRacer.tsx`) keeps the game-over-time board fetch — possibly still in flight on a stale SW — from clobbering the submit response, which once blanked a fresh #1 on the ALL tab). **Qualification**: the game-over initials form is gated on cracking a top-10 in ANY of the four period windows — at game over all four boards are fetched fresh into `qualifyBoards` (`TwingoRacer.tsx`) and the check ORs over them, so a 24H #1 earns its initials even while the ALL tab is open (the visible tab's board only drives the list display; a failed/absent qualify fetch stays optimistic — the server plausibility checks are the real gate). The store file carries a `version` field — a version mismatch wipes scores but keeps nonces (bumped to 2 when the scoring model changed and to 3 for the difficulty squeeze, to 4 for the ×0.5 score rescale, to 5 for the 3-heart damage ladder + hot-chain boost economy; the manual alternative is deleting the file in the Dokploy volume). Submissions need a single-use HMAC token signed with `HIGHSCORE_SALT` (server-only env, never `NEXT_PUBLIC_*`) plus score/duration plausibility; initials are filtered by `lib/profanity.ts`. A valid token IS the anti-spam proof: the per-IP submit rate limit only counts FAILED attempts (a first-time submitter behind a shared IP no longer eats a 429); GET has its own generous 600/15 min bucket. The service worker never answers these APIs: `next.config.js` runtimeCaching puts a `NetworkOnly` route for `/api/highscore` + `/api/stocks` BEFORE the generic `/api/` NetworkFirst rule (24 h cache, 10 s network timeout) — that fallback once served a stale ALL board over a fresh #1 on a flaky connection, while the never-cached period tabs showed it fine. Without `HIGHSCORE_SALT` the API answers 503 and the game hides the leaderboard. Caching end-to-end: the route itself answers `Cache-Control: no-store`, `middleware.ts` no longer stamps ANY `Cache-Control` on `/api/*` (its blanket `public, max-age=300, s-maxage=3600` used to override the route's no-store — browsers replayed minutes-old boards over a fresh submit, the third incarnation of the "ALL hides my #1" bug), and the client fetches with `cache: "no-store"` too; the middleware matcher also excludes `sw.js`/`workbox-*.js` so the PWA script registers instead of 307-ing into `/en/sw.js`. Submit rejections and successful saves are logged to the server console with client IP, status, reason, and payload. Docker: `/app/data` is chowned to `nextjs` — mount a volume there in Dokploy or scores reset on redeploy. The privacy page has a matching `privacy.leaderboard` section in all 13 locales.
- **Twingo Racer brackets**: LoL-style score tiers in `components/retro/racer/brackets.ts` (single source: `RACER_BRACKETS` descending by `min` score + colour, `bracketForScore`/`nextBracket`). Eşikler: IRON <10k, BRONZE 10k, SILVER 20k, GOLD 35k, PLATINUM 50k, EMERALD 60k, DIAMOND 70k, MASTER 85k, GRANDMASTER 100k (the marquee number), CHALLENGER 120k (≈65 km ≈ 25 dk tam gaz — anchor "25+ dk = tanrı seviyesi"); scores accrue at ×0.5 (metres × speed multiplier × 0.5). Derived from the score, no API/storage change: the game-over overlay shows the full bracket badge + "NEXT: X — n TO GO" line, leaderboard rows (title + game-over, all period tabs) carry a league-coloured diamond gem (`.racer-lb-gem` — a crisp-edges pixel SVG diamond via the `BracketGem` component, `shape-rendering: crispEdges`; the old rotated square anti-aliased at the fractional offsets of the panel's translate(-50%,-50%) centering and rows visibly wobbled — inside a fixed-width `.racer-lb-gem-wrap` column, and the row itself is a fixed 3-col grid (`grid-template-columns: 8ch 12px 6ch` + `justify-content: center`) — ALL columns MUST stay fixed-width: with auto/`1fr` width a 4-digit score narrowed its row and the gem drifted right on exactly those rows, and a flexible name column stranded the whole block left of centre with short initials (the last two "kayık"s, both verified 0 px spread / 0 px centre delta via Playwright measurements); the wrapper also holds a pure-CSS retro tooltip, `::after` from `data-bracket` with a bracket-coloured border, gated to `@media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)` so touch never sees it — no native `title` bubble, `aria-label` kept). Thresholds pair with the drainGain squeeze so each step up demands a visibly lower can-miss rate (see the economy calibration in the distance-difficulty note); re-reviewed after the 3-heart ladder + hot-chain boost landed — KEPT as-is: the heart rule shortens mid-tier runs while the boost uptime buff inflates the top end, and the two effects cancel at the anchor points. **Bracket reward**: the engine imports `RACER_BRACKETS` and tracks the current tier (`bracketIdx`, index only ever climbs — each tier pays ONCE per run): crossing into the next tier grants +1 heart back if one is missing (`crashes--` — the crumpled chassis keeps its permanent speed loss) or, at full hearts, a **streak shield** with `SHIELD_MAX` (3) charges; every streak break (a missed ACTIVE can AND a crash alike) is eaten for one charge instead of breaking the chain, flashing a small `SHIELD!` popup by the HUD can. The crossing fires a 1.8 s centre banner in the tier's own colour (`BRONZE! +1 HEART` / `GOLD! SHIELD x3`, parked at 0.48 h — below the record banner's 0.42 h slot) plus a rising major fanfare (`bracket()` in `audio.ts`, `onBracket`). The shield shows as a pulsing yellow-green guardian ring around the HUD jerrycan with one pip per charge on its top-right arc (the bare can stands even on a cold chain while a shield is armed, so the ring has something to guard).
