- **Twingo Racer audio**: `components/retro/racer/audio.ts` synthesizes everything with raw Web Audio (no assets). The engine voice is a Mk1 D7F firing model — 1149 cc inline-4 four-stroke, f = RPM/30 (28 Hz idle → 200 Hz redline; sub sine + main saw + dominant 2nd harmonic + phase-locked sub-octave saw through a resonant lowpass into a throttle-load-driven waveshaper for growl, plus filtered-noise rumble); lifting off at revs fires bandpassed overrun-crackle pops, and at the gear top with the throttle pinned a rev limiter stutters the ignition in an irregular cut pattern; `EngineState.rpm01` is wheel-speed over the gear's top, so upshifts drop revs by the ratio gap and downshifts kick them up on their own — the 0.28 s shift cut (`GEAR_TOPS`/`SHIFT_TIME` in `engine.ts`, committed gear + aero-only coast during the cut) only ducks load and fires a clutch thump. Acceleration follows the real D7F (0-100 in ~13.4 s, ~20 s more to 150), with an arcade tail to the 180 speedo ceiling. Bandpass-noise brake screech and tire skid (`EngineState.skid`/`braking`), and a synthwave loop that adds layers by score (lead ≥12.5k, octave doubler ≥30k, double-time hats ≥50k (halved with the ×0.5 score scale)). - **Twingo Racer distance difficulty**: every 1.5 km (`LEVEL_EVERY_KM` — a tight ladder so the ×1.5 knee arrives by ~12 km while the drain cap (×1.9) only completes near ~64 km; older records predate this pacing and were wiped at store v3) bumps `state.level` in `components/retro/racer/engine.ts` (uncapped) and shows a brief 2.2 s `LEVEL X` canvas flash with the level's fuel-consumption multiplier underneath (`FUEL xN.NN` in the same face, accent orange — same font and size, no backing band). Difficulty applies through pure multipliers on the generated track data (never mutated): `curveGain` (+8%/level, cap 1.6) sharpens every bend and `hillGain` (+7%/level, cap 1.5) steepens hills — both must be applied at EVERY `.curve` / `world.y` read site (projection, mirror, background parallax) or the world tears. Hill sections are grade-capped in `track.ts` (`MAX_GRADE = 0.35` on the stored cosine-eased peak — sections STRETCH to keep amplitude, never flatten): past ~0.8 a descent compresses into a marking-less sliver at the screen edge, past ~1.2 the far side fails the backface test wholesale and the road vanishes behind the crest (the "yol boşalıyor" bug), and `hillGain` multiplies on top — 0.35 stored keeps the effective grade ≤ ~0.5 at max gain, worst-case road band ~28% of the screen. a `min(1, 3p)` gate keeps a parked car from sliding sideways. **Crest hop & landing grip**: clearing a hilltop above 87 km/h (armed by a steep climb <0.9 s earlier) pops the car airborne for ~0.3-0.6 s (`airT`/`airDur`;
